          How it is calculated

          Monthly saving = (workers now − workers with machine) × monthly salary − extra running costs. Staff kept on payroll give no wage saving; additional workers still add salary cost.

          Manual team output = current employees × manual output per employee per hour. Machine output is the total for one machine. Time for 1,000 pieces = 1,000 ÷ team hourly output × 60 minutes. Rates are planning assumptions until checked for the customer’s product.

          Payback months = (machine price + extra one-time costs) ÷ positive monthly saving. The estimate assumes consistent savings and excludes finance or any costs you have not entered.
